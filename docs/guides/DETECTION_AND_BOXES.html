Production tab: detection and bounding boxes

What the YOLO detector shows on the Production tab, when, and what it does not do. Colours and timings come from the code (infer.py, machine_cycle.py, gui.py).

Status, stated plainly: the box drawing is built and passes software self-tests with a fake detector. It has not yet been shown to find a real bottle on your conveyor: the YOLO model was trained on 594 images from another setup. Treat the first boxes you see on the machine as the test.

3. What the detector decides and what it does not

It does
  • Finds bottle, cap and label and draws them.
  • Says whether a bottle is present (an empty belt reads NO BOTTLE).
  • Feeds the decision engine: a required part (cap, label) missing in most of the bottle's frames becomes a defect through the vote.
It does not
  • Judge damage, tilt, water level or skew: the classifier does that.
  • Fire a reject on one frame or one missing box (occlusion, blur and glare also hide parts).
  • Talk to the PLC. Only the decision engine's result does.

5. Quick test (2 minutes, no PLC)

  1. Production tab, engineer mode: press START INSPECTION.
  2. Hold a bottle upright in front of camera 1, label toward the lens.
  3. Expected: blue bottle box, green cap box, yellow label box within about half a second. Remove the bottle: the boxes vanish and the caption reads NO BOTTLE.
  4. No boxes? Note what the Health page MODELS card and the pane caption say. Wrong boxes? Press Capture test frame so the image can be used to retrain.

6. If the boxes are poor on the real belt

That is expected for a model trained elsewhere. Capture 100–200 frames on the real conveyor (Capture test frame), open the Annotate tab, Propose boxes, accept or correct them, and train the detector on them. Your own enclosure images, with your lighting, belt and bottle, improve it most.
