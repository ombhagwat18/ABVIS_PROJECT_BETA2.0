Camera placement in the enclosure

Enclosure 450 × 320 × 400 mm (L × W × H, your numbers). Conveyor 5 ft in about 15 s ≈ 100 mm/s. Change any value on the left; the drawing and the settings update. Defaults for the bottle and camera field of view are assumptions to be measured, not facts.

Two things to measure before trusting any number here: (1) the camera's real field of view: hold a ruler at a known distance and read how wide and how tall the picture is, then FOV = 2·atan(width / 2 / distance). (2) The belt speed: time a marked bottle over a measured length.

Placement rules

ItemDoWhy
Two camerasOpposite walls, B staggered along the belt by more than the number shown above.Neither camera sees the other, no lens glints in frame, each sees its half of the bottle. About 240° of good coverage, not 360°.
HeightOptical axis at mid-bottle, camera level.Cap, label and the water line stay in frame without tilt distortion.
OrientationPortrait (rotate 90°): camera_controls "rotate": 90 in settings.json.A tall bottle fills the long side of the frame; the model input is tall.
LightingTwo 6500 K strips above each camera, angled down 30–45°, diffused; DC (non-PWM) supply.No hard streak on the PET; no flicker bands.
BackgroundMatte black behind each camera's bottle (the opposite wall), black floor, hood around each lens.Clean edges; no reflections.
Focus / exposureLock by hand at the bottle position, exposure as short as the light allows (blur shown above).A drifting autofocus changes the image bottle to bottle.
USBEach camera on its own USB port (different root hub).Two cameras on one USB 2.0 hub gave only one 1080p stream.

After mounting (in this order)

  1. Production tab → CAMERA TEST: bottle fully in view, focus sharp.
  2. Set ROI…: drag a rectangle around where the bottle passes. The grey/yellow rectangle then shows on the live pane; bottles whose centre is outside it are ignored.
  3. Arrival timing…: send 5+ bottles one at a time. The tool times X0 → bottle visible in the ROI → bottle gone, and proposes capture_delay_s and inspect_window_s. Apply. This replaces the calculation above with a measurement.
  4. Time the bottle from the photo-eye to the reject cylinder (stopwatch) and put it in reject_travel_s; set the ladder's T0 below (travel − delay − window − 0.4 s).
  5. TEST INSPECTION with a good and a bad bottle, then START INSPECTION.
